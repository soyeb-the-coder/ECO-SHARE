The Crisis

India wastes 67 million tonnes of food every year

Enough to feed every malnourished person in the country three times over. Meanwhile, food rotting in landfills produces methane — 25x more potent than CO₂ — a wasted energy source that no one is capturing.

The Solution

Introducing the Zero-Waste Cascade

Every kg of food finds its highest purpose — people, animals, or fuel. Nothing reaches landfill.

🍽️

Feed People

Edible surplus food is matched with volunteers, orphanages, and communities in under 30 minutes.

🐾

Feed Animals

Stale rotis, veg peels, overripe fruits — routed to shelters, Gaushalas, and street feeders.

⚡

Biogas Fuel

Truly rotten waste goes to anaerobic digesters → biomethane → runs vehicles & generators.

🌱

Compost

Nothing wasted — the final residue becomes nutrient-rich compost for campus gardens.

Component Architecture

Four layers. One closed loop.

From waste bin to biogas pump — every kg is sensed, routed, and tracked end-to-end.

Layer 1 · Sensing (bin-side)
Ultrasonic HC-SR04 reads fill level, HX711 load cell weighs the bin, DHT11 tracks temperature & humidity for food safety, MQ-4 detects methane leaks.

Layer 2 · Connectivity
ESP32's onboard Wi-Fi streams readings over HTTPS to Firebase — with offline queuing and deep-sleep for low power.

Layer 3 · Cascade Intelligence
Rule engine decides every kg's fate — edible → people, animal-safe → shelters, rotten → biogas. Temperature & expiry logic auto-triggers pickups.
